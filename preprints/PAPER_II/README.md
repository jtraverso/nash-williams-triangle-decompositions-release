## Frozen formalization

The selected archive is [PAPER_II_LEAN_20261020_ALIGNED_v4.zip](../../archives/PAPER_II_LEAN_20261020_ALIGNED_v4.zip): 1,510 project modules, fifteen standard-axiom entry queries, and a reversible header correction over the completed clean aligned campaign. All author headers identify Juan Pablo Traverso Gianini under Apache-2.0. Historical receipts and logs are unchanged; the companion explains the distinction between the compiled and header-corrected bytes.

The campaign compiled 623 shared modules in its Paper I phase and 887 in its Paper II phase. The [Paper I archive](../PAPER_I/README.md) retains its separate 1,070-module closure and six final queries. Both papers now use the same four-colour bridge. This is local campaign evidence, not an independent reproduction.
