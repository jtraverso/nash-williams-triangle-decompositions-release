# Style, exposition and series fit — preprint 1.0

The opening gives the problem, sufficient conditions and T before the
construction. T+ states one simultaneous decomposition. Applications share one
section. The terminology, family acknowledgment, tool disclosure and typesetting
preambles follow Papers I and II.

The new explanations identify inputs before using an inverse, separate the
integer and real conditions, list the final assembly objects, derive the signed
factor five, derive the Pasch rate budget and explain the cubic recurrence. Long
local existence proofs are expressly assigned to the formal source. No slogan
about a standard counting argument is used to claim those local proofs are
printed in full.

The abstract is divided into short paragraphs. Spanish uses the target language
for the exposition and keeps source identifiers unchanged. Tests are called
test functions (funciones de prueba), avoiding ambiguity with mathematical
proofs. The profile determines the intercalate target. Known existence results
and simultaneous statistical conclusions are separated.

No claim is made that the text was written without AI or that it can evade
detectors. The series disclosure remains intact. No invented numerical style
score or independent human review is reported. This is an internal editorial
assessment for the first public preprint, not a journal acceptance forecast.
