## Acceptance conditions

1. Preserve every theorem-statement paragraph and all 72 numbered equation tags.
   Preserve every tagged display except the documented reorganization of D.5.
2. Check bilingual paragraph mathematics, identifiers, headings, figures and
   tables; explain all semantic changes and confirm agreement with source.
3. Trace every Lean file and declaration cited in prose to the packaged module
   inventory and to the appropriate target import closure. Distinguish explicit
   quantitative variants from the variants used by T.
4. Verify the real-image and support conditions for exactification, sparse edge
   persistence, signed-envelope mass bound, constant-choice order, fixed-profile
   iteration, contraction, monotone Pasch lower bound and upper growth budget.
5. Recalculate finite identities and the thirty-triangle certificate exactly.
   These checks are regression evidence, not substitutes for universal proofs.
6. Revalidate the completed 1,141-module record and object hashes; prove that
   source cleanups affect comments alone; recompile those 15 modules and rerun
   the six endpoint axiom queries. Admit only propext, Classical.choice and
   Quot.sound. State explicitly that cache reuse is not a fresh empty-cache build.
7. Check bibliography against primary sources and delimit the comparison with
   Delcourt–Postle, including the differing printed hinge exponent. Do not claim
   an exhaustive novelty search or access to the unavailable separate manuscript.
8. Compile four PDFs from final delivered TeX; resolve all references; inspect
   every rendered page and compare the series layout. Check every figure with
   both PyMuPDF and Poppler, including PDF dash syntax.
9. Remove obsolete status and earlier-version references from all publication
   documents. Preserve historical evidence separately, without rewriting it.
10. Generate final reports and manifests only after all corrections. Check the
    sealed source archive, the complete package and its publication copy.

PASS is the verdict of this internal audit within the scope above. It is not a
claim of independent human peer review, optimal novelty, a complete standalone
prose reproduction of the formal library, or a public repository release.
