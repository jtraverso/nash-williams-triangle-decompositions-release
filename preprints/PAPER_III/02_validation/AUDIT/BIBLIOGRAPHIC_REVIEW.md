## Closest announced criterion

Delcourt–Postle, *A Proof of Nash-Williams' Conjecture*,
[arXiv:2606.11178v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.11178v1), Section 13.2,
Definitions 13.10–13.15 and Theorem 13.16, is the closest explicit statement.
The manuscript now compares the hypotheses separately. H3 is the typed
anti-edgeful condition; the hinge is attributed to Definition 13.14. The exact
positive seed is stronger than a regular almost-packing. A physical integer
certificate implies the aggregate integral divisibility conditions, and the
seed supplies fractional divisibility by summation.

Definition 13.15 prints a power with two fewer fixed roots than the normalization
used here. The article states this discrepancy and explains the four-root count.
It does not silently replace the printed condition or claim an author-confirmed
erratum. Consequently it describes T as an independently proved formal variant,
not a literal corollary or a strict generalization of the printed theorem.

The proof is referred in that source to a separate manuscript. The dated title
search did not locate a public copy of that separate work. This bounded absence
check is distinguished from access to its proof. The checked date appears in
both languages. The octahedral hinge is attributed to their sphere construction
and to Keevash's octahedral exchanges.

## Scope relative to other results

Keevash's *The existence of designs II*,
[arXiv:1802.05900v1](https://arxiv.org/html/1802.05900v1), Theorem 3.1 and
Section 5.1, and *Coloured and directed designs*,
[arXiv:1807.05770v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1807.05770v2), Theorem 4.18,
provide the labelled-complex context. No implication from H1–H4 to all their
extendability and regularity requirements was established in this editorial
audit. The preprint therefore makes no greater-generality or priority claim on
that basis.

The Latin-square literature already gives the leading intercalate count near
one quarter of the square of the order. The application is framed as joint
control with preselected linear tests. Its intercalate target is determined by
the seed profile. The five-partite existence witness already supplies a
decomposition; T+ adds the statistical conclusion. Neither application is
presented as a new existence theorem in isolation.

Postle is the sole author of reference [7]. The versioned arXiv links, DOI
metadata for the journal papers, and the three series deposits were checked
against the archived primary records. Software references describe tools, not
unproved mathematical inputs.

## Editorial disposition

The attribution and positioning observations are resolved within this stated
scope. No claim of historical priority, literature exhaustiveness or independent
expert review is certified. Any assessment of the novelty of the combined
statistical conclusion remains open to the external referees' mathematical
judgment, rather than being encoded as an internal PASS on priority.
